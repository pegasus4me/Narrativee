# Creative quality benchmark

Status: protocol prepared; no new live results or human ratings recorded yet.

## Compare three real briefs

1. GROUNDED chocolate flavor launch: supplied real carton and current logo; Instagram portrait; bold flavor announcement. Failure conditions: generic glass substitution, altered packaging or label, misspelled copy, competing text panels.
2. A software task announcement: supplied real screenshot and logo; LinkedIn landscape. Failure conditions: invented dashboard features or metrics, unreadable interface, hierarchy that hides the core message.
3. A service campaign: actual brand materials and a specific audience/use case; square output. Failure conditions: invented claims, irrelevant imagery, generic slogan that obscures the offering.

Use a real participating brand for cases 2 and 3, not invented evidence. Save the same brief, reference IDs, selected format and model settings for each comparison. Compare the previous direction with the new recipe/reference workflow; make three generations per brief only when the owner has authorized the generation budget. Include the first draft and any final correction in the record. Keep results as private project assets.

## Human review

Show results in randomized order without revealing which pipeline made them. Ask the brand owner and one independent designer:

- Is the real product/logo preserved? Pass/fail with a specific defect.
- Is all required copy correct and legible? Pass/fail with a specific defect.
- Does it honor approved brand rules? Pass/fail with a specific conflict.
- Does the focal point and reading order work at feed size? Rate 1–5.
- Do the imagery, materials, lighting and typography look intentional? Rate 1–5.
- Would you publish this unchanged, publish after a small correction, or reject it?

Aesthetic ratings are judgments, not proof of ad performance. A proposed launch target is at least 8 of 10 creatives publishable without rebuilding and no wrong-product or required-copy errors. This target has not been achieved or measured.
