# Narrativee: first-customer prospects for static creatives

Researched and updated 14 October 2026 through treg. Global scope. **10 prioritized prospects, 3 additional product brands to qualify, 15 agency candidates on hold and 2 excluded. None contacted.** Current need and willingness to pay remain unconfirmed.

## Targeting constraint

**Static creatives only:** branded image ads, social graphics and product promotional images. Exclude agencies centered on TikTok Shop, UGC video or video production. Juicy Creative and Factor have been removed from outreach. Remaining generalist agencies must bring a specific recurring static brief; their video or platform-management services are not the proposed use case. The other 15 agency candidates are on hold until their static production work is checked.

The ICP follows Narrativee's current [README](../../README.md): an existing brand, source images, recurring static briefs, and a buyer who can approve work and pay. Four agencies and six direct product brands make up this first group. Product-brand creative frequency is a hypothesis to qualify, rather than an observed complaint.

Pitch a founder-guided workflow: product image + brief → generation → corrections → PNG export. Use manually saved, approved brand rules where supported. Verify the served workflow before offering a self-serve trial. Company sizes are provider estimates.

## A concrete route to 10 paying customers

1. Start with the four remaining agencies: All Over Social, Little Media Agency, WAY Digital and Clickstera. Qualify only a recurring static-image brief. Send the individual opener through the checked route only after reviewing it. Ask for a real brief they need soon, rather than opinions about AI.
2. During the conversation, qualify recurring static-image volume, current production time, revision bottlenecks, ownership of source images, who approves, and buying authority. A brand match without a repeated job is not enough.
3. Run one genuine brief with founder support and ask whether they would actually publish the result. Record customer minutes, corrections, outside design cleanup and whether they use the export.
4. Request a second genuine brief for the same brand. Save approved rules manually where supported and compare repeated briefing/correction effort. Do not claim automatic learning that is not shipped.
5. If both briefs are useful, offer paid continuation at the currently available, verified plan terms. The repo currently lists Starter $19/month and Pro $49/month; these are configured prices, not a confirmed live checkout or a promised number of campaign packages.
6. Count a customer only after payment and real use. Work through the additional product brands and qualify the agency hold queue for static work, expanding the queue until 10 pay; 10 prospects cannot be assumed to yield 10 customers. There is no measured response or close rate yet.

Suggested follow-up cadence: one brief follow-up after 3–4 business days and one after roughly a week, then stop if there is no response. Do not use a community posting or mass-message campaign to replace qualification. No outreach or recurring automation was created in this research task.

Keep TikTok, UGC video and video production outside the offer and targeting.

Track per prospect: owner, contact channel, first-message date, response, qualified weekly job, first brief, actually used export, second brief, paid amount/date and reason lost. All entries currently remain **Not contacted**.
